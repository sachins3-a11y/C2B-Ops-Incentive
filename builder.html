Upload one CSV. This works out each hub's targets, the checks and the payout, then gives you hubs.json for the repo. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Documents received — one bar for every hub.
Documents dispatched — one bar for every hub.
Dealer complaints — per 100 cars stocked out.
Yard app — where it isn't available the hub runs 3 checks, each worth a third.

Columns: hub, league, heads, stock_out, doc_received_pct, doc_dispatched_pct, dealer_tickets, yms_pct, yms_available.

League comes from the CSV (A / B / C). stock_out is the monthly figure — the quarter is worked out from the window and quarter day counts below. Hubs with no headcount are listed as left out rather than silently dropped.
